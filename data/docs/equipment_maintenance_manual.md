# Sentinel AI — Equipment Maintenance Manual

## Overview

This manual covers maintenance procedures for industrial equipment in Zone A and Zone B
of the manufacturing facility. All maintenance schedules must be followed precisely
to avoid equipment failure and downtime.

---

## Section 1: Pump Maintenance Schedule

### Pump Model: Acme A7 Centrifugal Pump

**Maintenance Frequency**: Every 2000 operating hours or 3 months, whichever comes first.

**Procedure:**
1. Shut off power supply and lock out/tag out (LOTO) the pump.
2. Drain the pump casing and remove inlet/outlet fittings.
3. Inspect impeller for wear, cavitation damage, and debris.
4. Check mechanical seal for leaks — replace if wear exceeds 0.3mm.
5. Grease bearings with Mobil Grease XHP 222 (two pumps per bearing).
6. Inspect motor coupling alignment — maximum allowable offset: 0.1mm.
7. Replace intake filter cartridge (Part #: AF-2201).
8. Restart and monitor pressure (target: 45–55 PSI), flow rate (target: 120 L/min).

**Critical Alarm Thresholds:**
- Vibration > 5 mm/s: Immediate shutdown
- Temperature > 85°C: Alert maintenance team
- Pressure drop > 20%: Check for blockage

## Section 2: Compressor Maintenance

### Model: Atlas Copco GA 55 Rotary Screw Compressor

**Weekly Checks:**
- Oil level (maintain between MIN and MAX marks)
- Condensate drain operation
- Air filter condition (clean if pressure drop exceeds 50 mbar)

**6-Month Service:**
1. Replace air/oil separator element.
2. Change oil (use Atlas Copco Roto-Inject Fluid grade).
3. Clean cooler fins with compressed air.
4. Inspect all belts and hoses for cracking.
5. Calibrate pressure switch ±2%.

**Emergency Procedures:**
- High temperature shutdown: Check cooling fan, clean cooler, verify oil level.
- Oil carry-over in air: Replace separator element immediately.

---

## Section 3: Safety Procedures

All maintenance must be performed by certified technicians wearing:
- Hard hat (ANSI Z89.1)
- Safety glasses (ANSI Z87.1)
- Steel-toed boots (ASTM F2413)
- Hearing protection (NRR 25 dB minimum) in compressor room

**LOTO procedures are mandatory before any mechanical work.**
Failure to follow LOTO procedures is grounds for immediate disciplinary action.
